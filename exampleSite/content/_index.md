Rooted in the sounds of Batavia, the journey began far from the stage—forged as a bus conductor and a soldier. The resulting art always centered on representing the common people and marginalized voices.

A legacy of over 75 albums and 50 films. A pioneer in blending traditional *gambang kromong* with modern jazz and blues. From hits like "Ondel-Ondel" to Citra Award-winning cinema, the work remains dedicated to immortalizing the Betawi spirit.
